# Example data of `fde demo`

`sfts_mortality.csv.gz`: S1 Data of a hospital cohort with severe fever with thrombocytopenia syndrome, 2017–2024,
PLOS figshare (https://api.figshare.com/v2/articles/29130855), licence CC BY 4.0. One row per patient (baseline sheet),
target `class`: in-hospital death (`died`) or not (`survived`); ICU transfer and length of stay removed. The same table
is one of the 53 evaluation datasets of the accompanying paper (`benchmark/data_open/sfts-mortality.csv.gz`).
